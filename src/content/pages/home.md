## What is Immigration Archive?

First of all, TravelCrew is a kind of digital humanities project dedicated to studying the immigration and refugee entry patterns in the US. ANd the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) to analyze the changes in immigration patterns over time and the differences between these patterns across various states.

Through data visualization, maps, and historical context, this project aims to help people better understand the complex immigration statistics. At the same time, we also explore which information the government data can reveal and which aspects of people's own experiences might have been overlooked. Besides that, immigration has played a significant role in the development of the US. However, the patterns of immigration are not static; they change with government policies, international events, and public health emergencies.

Our research is based on data from the Office of Immigration Statistics of the Department of Homeland Security (OHSS), specifically the refugee entry data from the fiscal years 2013 to 2024. For instance, during the fiscal years 2016 to 2018, the number of refugee entries in California, Texas, and New York decreased significantly. By comparing the situations in different years and states, we explored how these trends changed during the terms of successive presidents and during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Although official statistics help us identify trends, they do not fully reflect the personal experiences of immigrants and refugees.

## Research Questions

1. How did refugee admissions across U.S. states change after the transition from the Obama administration to the Trump administration in 2017, and which states saw the largest declines?
2. How closely does a state's admissions track its political leaning from 2013 to 2022, and did Republican vs Democratic leaning states drift further apart after 2017?
3. To what extent did annual refugee and asylee admissions differ between administrations, and how did the 2020 COVID-19 pandemic complicate that comparison?
