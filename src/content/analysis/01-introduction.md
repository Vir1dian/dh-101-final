## Background of Immigration Policies: Obama vs. Trump

*By Francesca He*

The changes in immigration policies provide an important backdrop for understanding the immigration trends in the United States. Puiu (2023, pp. 180–182) discusses the differences between the Obama and Trump administrations in handling undocumented immigrants, border security, and cooperation with Latin American countries.

According to Puiu's analysis, the Obama administration implemented border enforcement while also taking measures to protect some undocumented immigrants and supporting immigration reform. Its policies also emphasized cooperation with Latin American countries to address issues related to immigration-related poverty and violence. In contrast, the Trump administration placed more emphasis on restrictive policies, physical border barriers, and pressuring other governments to cooperate with the United States' immigration enforcement actions.

For TravelCrew, this comparison provides a framework for discussing the political environment in which the immigration trends are situated in the data, and prompts us to consider how the government's policy priorities affect the opportunities and obstacles faced by immigrants. However, changes in immigration numbers alone cannot prove that a particular policy led to such changes. Economic conditions, family ties, and the circumstances of the countries of origin of immigrants may also affect immigration flows. Therefore, we will consider the policy changes as historical background and distinguish between the trends observable in the data and explanations that require additional evidence support.
